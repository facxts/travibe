# Travibe

Travibe is a travel exploration app that pairs music with discovery. Instead of just showing you a map, it turns exploring a new destination into an experience — play music that matches the vibe of a place while browsing an interactive map to find spots you didn't know existed.

## Meaning of "Travibe"

It's a combination of Travel + Vibes. It captures the core idea of the app pretty well — it's not just about navigating a map to get from place to place, it's about the vibe of traveling: the music, the mood, the feeling of discovering somewhere new. The name basically promises "travel with a vibe to it."

## What it does

- **Explore new places** — Discover destinations you've never been to through an interactive map interface.
- **Music while you explore** — Curated tracks play alongside your exploration, so discovering a new place feels less like research and more like a trip.
- **AI local guide** — An AI-powered guide accompanies you as you explore, offering context and personality along the way.

## Drive Map

Drop a pin anywhere on the map and Travibe shows you the place name and city right there, similar to how Google Maps works — so you can explore an area, click on something that catches your eye, and instantly know what it is and where it's located before deciding to check it out further.

### Controls

Drive Mode lets you move a car around the map like a simple driving game:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W** | Drive forward |
| **A** | Turn/steer left |
| **S** | Drive backward / brake |
| **D** | Turn/steer right |

As you drive around, nearby places pop up automatically — just like dropping a pin, but discovered as you go.

## The fun part

The AI guide dresses in the traditional clothing of whatever country or region you're currently exploring — a small, playful detail that makes each destination feel distinct as you move around the map.

## Known limitations

- The AI local guide is still a work in progress — it doesn't always understand or respond accurately to what you say. Conversational accuracy is an active area of improvement.

## Tech Stack

- React + TypeScript
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- Leaflet / MapLibre GL for mapping
